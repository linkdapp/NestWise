One more trap

A PDB has
no SID.

Every connection goes through a service name. The old Enterprise Manager connect descriptor named a SID that no longer starts.

Create it in the PDB, not the root

CREATE_SERVICE attaches the service to whichever container is current. Created in CDB$ROOT, the listener registers the name, the connection succeeds, and the session lands somewhere SYSMAN does not exist.
